01 — OVERVIEW

A magazine with visual tension.

The project explores how a restrained editorial system can create a strong identity without relying on excessive decoration.

Large type, asymmetric composition and deliberate whitespace create a rhythm that feels closer to a film sequence than a conventional magazine spread.
