NOIR — ISSUE 001

A visual journal of urban life. A magazine concept built around neon streets, night drives and the quiet tension of a city that never really sleeps.

ART DIRECTION · EDITORIAL · TYPOGRAPHY
01 — OVERVIEW

The city is alive.

NOIR is an editorial experiment about the visual language of a city after dark. Neon, old cars, reflections and blurred lights become the raw material for a cinematic magazine identity.

The cover uses oversized vertical typography against a deliberately imperfect photograph. The closer you look, the more details appear.

The city is alive.
